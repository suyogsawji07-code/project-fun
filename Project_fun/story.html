Our Friendship

A short story of two people who somehow never got tired of each other.

It started like most good friendships do — without any plan. No big moment, no grand meeting. Just two people who kept ending up in the same conversations, the same jokes, and eventually, the same corner of each other's lives.

Since then, it's been a mix of everything. We've supported each other on the hard days, without needing to be asked. We've fought over the silliest things and forgotten why within a few hours. We've laughed for no reason at all, the kind of laughing that doesn't need a joke to start it.

We've shared problems we didn't tell anyone else. We've trusted each other with the messy, unfiltered version of ourselves — and neither of us ran away.

That's really the whole story. Nothing dramatic. Just a friendship that quietly became one of the best parts of my life.
